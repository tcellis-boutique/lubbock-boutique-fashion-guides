Western boho fashion in Lubbock combines relaxed bohemian styling with classic Texas-inspired fashion trends. Around Texas Tech University and throughout West Texas boutique culture, many shoppers mix flowy dresses, cowboy boots, layered jewelry, denim pieces, and earthy neutral tones to create effortless everyday outfits.
